# Calibration

- DARK = 14800
- BRIGHT = 47000
- Averaging samples (n) = 16

## How I measured

- DARK: I covered the LDR completely with my finger and used a typical value from the flicker.
- BRIGHT: I held a phone flashlight close to the LDR and used a typical value.
- Averaging: I chose n = 16 because it steadies the reading, but it reacts a little slower.
